          How to play

          One frame a day from a film, the same frame for everyone. It starts badly out of focus.

          Name the film by picking it from the list. A wrong guess or a skip uses a take and pulls focus a little further. You have six takes; the sixth is the sharp frame.

          Typos never cost a take: if a title is not in the list, nothing is spent.

          A new frame arrives at midnight, your time.
