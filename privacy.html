Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Frame Zero is a daily film guessing game, made and run as a personal project by Atharv Gopaluni. In short: you can play without an account, we keep only what the game needs to check your takes and keep score, and nothing is ever sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

Playing as a guest

  • So the server can check each take fairly, your browser gets an anonymous guest id from Supabase. It carries no name, no email and nothing about your device.
  • For each day you play we keep one record against that id: the date, the titles you tried, whether you solved it, how many takes it took, and when it was last updated.
  • Delete my data in the Account panel deletes the guest id and every record. Clearing this site's data in your browser also cuts the link; the orphaned records hold nothing that identifies you.

If you sign in with Google

  • Sign-in goes through Google and Supabase Auth. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Google shares your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture link. Supabase Auth keeps these to run your account. The game itself uses only the identifier, and shows your email in the Account panel.
  • Your games are kept the same way as a guest's, so your streak and stats follow you to every device.
  • When you first sign in, the games you played as a guest in that browser move into your account. Nothing already in your account is overwritten.
  • A sign-in session token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

The leaderboard

  • The leaderboard is opt-in. You appear on it only if you choose a username.
  • It shows your username and your scores: monthly points, streak, days played, and which take you solved today's frame on. It never shows your email or your Google name.
  • You can change your username once a week or leave the board at any time, which releases the name.
  • Usernames that are offensive or impersonate someone can be removed from the board.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Services involved

  • Supabase stores accounts and game records (Supabase privacy policy).
  • Google handles sign-in (Google privacy policy).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical request data such as IP addresses (GitHub privacy statement).

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept until you delete it.
  • Open the Account panel in the game and choose Delete my data. It deletes your account (guest or Google), your username and every game record straight away. It cannot be undone.
  • Signing out on its own keeps your account and records.

Children

Frame Zero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes
